About TransitPH

Making commuting easier, faster, and smarter for every Filipino.

TransitPH is a community-driven mobile app designed to help commuters find the best routes, terminals, and fare estimates across the Philippines. It aims to make public transportation simple, transparent, and accessible to everyone.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every Filipino commuter with accurate, real-time transit information. From confusing terminals to inconsistent fares, TransitPH helps solve everyday commuting problems by connecting local data with community-driven updates.

The Story

The project began as a student idea, inspired by the daily struggles of finding routes in unfamiliar cities. What started small has grown into a meaningful initiative to improve how Filipinos travel.

What Makes TransitPH Different

Unlike other navigation apps, TransitPH focuses on local transport systems—including jeepneys, tricycles, UV Express, and provincial routes—offering information that truly fits our daily commutes.

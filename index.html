Welcome to TransitPH

TransitPH is a community-driven mobile application that helps commuters find the best routes, terminals, and fare estimates across the Philippines. Whether you're a daily commuter or traveling to a new city, TransitPH makes your journey smoother by giving you reliable transit information right at your fingertips.

About TransitPH

Mission

Our mission is to simplify public transportation in the Philippines by making route information accessible, accurate, and user-friendly. We believe that better commute knowledge empowers every Filipino to save time, money, and energy.

The Story

TransitPH started as a school project that turned into a real-world solution after realizing how hard it is for commuters to find reliable route details. From confusing terminals to overpricing issues, we wanted to create a tool that solves these everyday problems.

Team & Developers

Developed by a passionate team of Filipino students who believe that technology can make daily commuting more convenient and transparent.
